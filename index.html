39+ Years of Tradition,
Trust & Timeless Style

"Trust is not given — it is earned, stitch by stitch, customer by customer."

— Late Shri Ujwal Rathi

Late Shri Ujwal Rathi founded Hiramoti Collection in 1987 at the historic heart of Satara with a singular promise: to provide the families of Satara, Wai, and Karad with exquisite apparel, uncompromising fabric quality, and genuine warmth.

From humble beginnings to our flagship two-story showroom opposite the famous Rajdhani Satara Selfie Point, that founding ethos remains the sacred heartbeat of everything we create.
