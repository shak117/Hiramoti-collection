Legal & Privacy Governance

Privacy Policy

Hiramoti Collection, Satara is committed to protecting your personal information and respecting your privacy across all digital and in-store touchpoints.

1. Information We Collect

When you interact with Hiramoti Collection (including booking showroom appointments, submitting style inquiries, connecting via WhatsApp, or registering an admin session), we may collect:

  • Full Name and contact details (WhatsApp number, phone number).
  • Area / city location within Satara and neighboring districts.
  • Style preferences, garment sizes, and showroom visit appointment dates.
  • Technical session identifiers and security timestamps strictly necessary for admin authentication.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your information exclusively to provide royal Satara hospitality and fulfill store appointments:

  • Preparing your requested garments and trials before you arrive at our Powai Naka showroom.
  • Dispatching WhatsApp visit confirmations and appointment reminders.
  • Authenticating authorized store administrators and safeguarding administrative records.
  • We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information with any third-party advertisers.

3. Data Security & Encryption

All administrative credentials and sensitive recovery tokens are cryptographically hashed using PBKDF2:SHA256 and stored in secure isolated database schemas. Communication between customer browsers and our backend is protected against unauthorized tampering.
